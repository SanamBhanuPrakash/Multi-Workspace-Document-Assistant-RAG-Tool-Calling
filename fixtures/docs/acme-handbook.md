# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Welcome to Acme Corp. This handbook covers the policies every employee should know. Acme Corp is headquartered in Lisbon, Portugal, with satellite offices in Porto and Berlin.

## Working hours and leave

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Lisbon time; outside those hours you may schedule your own day. Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave plus public holidays. Unused leave up to 5 days carries over into the first quarter of the next year. Sick leave is paid in full for the first 10 days per year and requires a note only after 3 consecutive days.

## Refund policy for customers

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of delivery. Physical goods must be returned unused in their original packaging, and the refund is issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of the return being received. Digital goods are refundable only if the license key has not been activated. Refunds above 500 euros require approval from a finance manager.

## Expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days through the finance portal. Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance in economy class. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day. The company card may not be used for personal purchases.

## Office security

The vault in the Lisbon office holds signing hardware and contract originals. The vault access code is ZEBRA-4417. It is rotated every quarter by the security lead and must never be sent by email or chat. Visitors must be signed in at reception and escorted at all times. Report lost badges to the security lead within one hour.
