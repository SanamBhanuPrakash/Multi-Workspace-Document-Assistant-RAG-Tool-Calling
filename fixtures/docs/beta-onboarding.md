# Beta Labs Engineering Onboarding

Beta Labs is a remote-first research company. This guide walks new engineers through their first month.

## Week one

New engineers receive a laptop on day one and are paired with a mentor for their first two weeks. By the end of week one you should have your development environment running, access to the monorepo, and your first small pull request merged. The team stand-up is at 09:30 UTC every weekday.

## Code review standards

Every change needs one approving review from a teammate outside the author's pair. Reviews should be answered within one working day. Pull requests over 400 changed lines should be split unless they are mechanical refactors. Tests are required for every bug fix.

## On-call rotation

The on-call rotation starts every Monday at 09:00 UTC and lasts one week. The on-call engineer carries the pager and responds to alerts within 15 minutes. The escalation phrase used to confirm a real sev-1 to the incident commander is "orange lantern". After a sev-1, the on-call engineer receives a compensation day.

## Learning budget

Each engineer has an annual learning budget of 1,500 dollars for books, courses and conferences. Conference travel needs manager approval in advance. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Equipment

Engineers may choose a laptop from the approved list and a monitor up to 34 inches. Equipment is replaced every 36 months. Return equipment within 14 days of leaving the company.
