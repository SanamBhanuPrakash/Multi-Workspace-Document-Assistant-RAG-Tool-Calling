# Acme Corp Information Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how Acme Corp protects customer and company information. It applies to all employees, contractors and systems.

## Access control

Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed every quarter. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate accounts. Shared accounts are prohibited. When an employee leaves the company, all access is revoked within 4 hours of their departure time.

## Password and secret handling

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are stored only in the approved password manager. Secrets such as API keys must never be committed to source control or pasted into chat tools. Suspected exposure of a secret must be reported to the security lead immediately, and the secret rotated within 24 hours.

## Incident response

Security incidents are classified as low, medium or high. High incidents require notification of the security lead within 30 minutes and of affected customers within 72 hours, in line with regulatory requirements. Every incident is followed by a written post-incident review within 10 working days.

## Data retention

Customer data is retained for 24 months after contract end and then securely deleted. Backups are encrypted and retained for 35 days. Logs containing personal data are retained for at most 90 days.

## Device policy

Laptops use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile application. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within one hour so they can be remotely wiped.
